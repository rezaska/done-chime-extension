Done Chime for Claude – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Canonical version: rezasoleimani.ca/done-chime/privacy

✓ This extension collects zero user data

Overview

Done Chime for Claude is a Chrome extension that plays a chime and shows a browser notification when Claude (claude.ai) finishes generating a response. It is a simple productivity utility with no accounts, no servers, and no data collection of any kind. Everything it does runs locally in your browser.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not read, store, or transmit the content of your Claude conversations.
  • We do not track browsing activity or history.
  • We do not use cookies or any form of analytics.
  • We do not share any data with third parties, because there is no data to share.

What the extension does

Done Chime for Claude runs entirely on your device. It watches for a specific UI change on claude.ai (the Stop button appearing and disappearing), which indicates that Claude has finished generating a response. When detected, it plays a locally synthesized audio chime and optionally shows a browser notification. No content from the page is read or recorded.

Permissions used

  • notifications – to show a browser notification when Claude finishes. This is optional and can be disabled in the extension popup.
  • storage – to save your preferences (volume, sound type, on/off state) on your device using local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Preferences are not synced to any cloud account and nothing is sent externally.
  • Host permission for claude.ai – required to inject the content script that watches for Claude's Stop button. The extension only activates on claude.ai pages.

The extension uses no remote code. All of its logic is bundled in the extension and runs entirely within your browser, so its behavior cannot change after install without an update you explicitly approve.

Data sovereignty & residency

The extension uses no servers, cloud services, databases, or third-party processors. No data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any server in any jurisdiction.

Because nothing ever leaves your device, your data does not reside in, and is never subject to the laws of, any country. There is no cross-border data transfer to consider, and no government request or data-access law could compel disclosure of your information. There is nothing to hold or disclose. The extension is developed in Canada by Reza Soleimani.

Your control over your data

  • Preferences live solely in your browser's local extension storage on your own device.
  • You can change or reset them at any time from the extension's popup.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage and all preferences along with it.

Children's privacy

This extension does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If the extension ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the extension version number will be incremented. You will be able to review any changes before updating.
